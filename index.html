02 — Selected Work

Things I've built

001

Network.me

Network.me is a LinkedIn connection visualizer that transforms your exported LinkedIn data into an interactive, searchable dashboard of your professional network. You can browse all your connections by company, job title, or custom tags, view when you connected, and track relationship context — like who referred you or who's a close collaborator. It also lets you add private notes to any connection and map relationships between people in your network. It's most useful for power networkers, recruiters, or anyone who wants a cleaner, more organized way to navigate their LinkedIn connections than LinkedIn itself provides.
